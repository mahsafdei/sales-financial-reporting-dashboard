# Sales & Financial Performance Reporting Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project simulates a real-world reporting workflow for a retail/distribution company.

The goal is to transform raw sales and financial data into meaningful management insights using Excel, SQL, and Power BI.

## Business Problem

Management needs a reliable monthly reporting solution to monitor sales performance, profitability, customer activity, product performance, and regional trends.

The objective of this project is to build an end-to-end reporting workflow that transforms raw transactional data into actionable management insights using Excel, SQL, and Power BI.

The analysis will focus on identifying revenue and profit trends, high-performing products and customers, regional performance differences, margin changes, and potential business risks.

## Business Questions

- How are revenue and gross profit changing over time?
- Which products and categories generate the most revenue and profit?
- Which customers contribute the most to total sales?
- Which regions are performing above or below average?
- Are higher sales always resulting in higher profitability?
- Which products have low margins despite strong sales?
- How does current performance compare with previous periods?
- What trends or exceptions should management investigate?

## Tools

- Microsoft Excel
- SQL
- Power BI
