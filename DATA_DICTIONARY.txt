DATA DICTIONARY

orders.csv
- OrderID: unique order-line identifier (duplicates were intentionally inserted for cleaning practice)
- OrderDate: transaction date
- CustomerID: customer key; a small number of blanks were intentionally inserted
- ProductID: product key
- Quantity: units sold; a small number of zero/negative values were intentionally inserted for data-quality investigation
- UnitPrice: selling price before discount
- DiscountPct: discount rate as decimal (0.10 = 10%)
- SalesChannel: Online, Direct, or Partner

customers.csv
- CustomerID: customer key
- CustomerName: customer display name
- Segment: Consumer, Corporate, Small Business
- City: customer city
- Region: Ontario, Quebec, West, Atlantic

products.csv
- ProductID: product key
- ProductName: product display name
- Category: Office Supplies, Technology, Furniture
- Subcategory: product subcategory
- UnitCost: unit product cost
- ListPrice: standard list price

monthly_budget.csv
- Month: first day of reporting month
- Region: reporting region
- RevenueBudget: monthly revenue budget
- GrossProfitBudget: monthly gross profit budget
